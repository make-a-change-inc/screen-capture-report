# Privacy, Security, Labour, and Legal POC Gates

The application implements technical privacy controls, but those controls are not legal approval. Before any real employee data is collected, the introducing company must record approval for every item below.

## Required approvals

- The documented purpose is workflow improvement only; appraisal, discipline, ranking, and unrelated secondary use are prohibited.
- Employees receive advance notice covering collected data, frequency, AI transmission, recipients, retention, deletion, pause/opt-out routes, correction, and incident contact.
- The company identifies an appropriate legal basis and completes any labour consultation or work-rule updates required in the deployment jurisdiction.
- Customer contracts and confidentiality duties permit the intended screen processing, or customer-confidential applications are excluded.
- Gemini account settings and contract terms covering training use, provider retention, subprocessors, and international transfer are reviewed and accepted.
- The employee, management, administrator, security, HR/labour, and legal owners are named. Management does not receive raw captures or raw titles.
- The POC company, department, participants, start/end dates, support route, and data-destruction date are recorded.

## Employee rights and operating procedure

- Capture state remains visible in the Windows tray.
- Active, paused, and explicit quit state is persisted so stopped periods are not reported as unclassified work.
- Employees can pause, quit, add exclusions, delete the current day's local capture payloads, inspect their daily report, and request correction of categories.
- Automatic capture is limited to configured work hours and stops on lock, idle, and excluded screens.
- An incident owner can pause the POC, preserve non-content audit evidence, notify affected parties, and execute deletion.
- POC end or participant departure triggers local data deletion and credential removal according to the approved schedule.

## Prohibited evidence shortcuts

- Synthetic tests do not prove legal approval, Gemini contractual controls, live classification accuracy, or live delivery.
- A mock, macOS run, PyInstaller spec, or static screenshot does not prove Windows runtime behavior.
- Missing measurements are reported as unmeasured; missing approval is reported as blocked for real-data use.
